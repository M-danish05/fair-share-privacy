Privacy Policy - Fair Share

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Introduction

Fair Share ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free offline group expense splitter application. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

Fair Share does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data you enter (groups, expenses, settlements) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Internet Access

Fair Share does not require or use an internet connection. The app functions entirely offline.

Third-Party Services

Fair Share does not integrate any third-party services, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash reporting frameworks.

Permissions

Fair Share does not request any special device permissions. The share feature uses your device's built-in share functionality to let you share expense summaries with others.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using an offline database. If you uninstall the app, all stored data will be permanently deleted.

Children's Privacy

Fair Share is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
